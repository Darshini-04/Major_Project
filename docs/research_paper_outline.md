# Research paper outline

1. Abstract: adaptive temporal customer behaviour analysis and retention decision support.
2. Introduction: limits of a static customer snapshot; research question and bounded contribution.
3. Related work: time-varying RFM and churn, e-commerce segmentation, interpretable churn, and LLM-based recommendation.
4. Dataset and preprocessing: Online Retail II, fields, date coverage, cancelled transactions, missing customer IDs, and cleaning decisions.
5. Methodology: temporal feature windows, RFM segmentation, next-segment target, future inactivity churn label, XGBoost and baselines, SHAP, and evidence-grounded recommendation generation.
6. Experimental design: chronological holdout, leakage controls, metrics, class imbalance, and ablations.
7. Results: pending measured experiments.
8. Discussion: empirical findings, business interpretation, limitations, and reproducibility.
9. Conclusion and future work: distinguish verified results from proposed capabilities.
